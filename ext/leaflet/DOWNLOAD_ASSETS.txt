Run bin/setup-assets.sh to download Leaflet 1.9.4 and
leaflet.markercluster 1.5.3 into this directory.

Files needed:
  leaflet.js, leaflet.css, images/marker-*.png
  leaflet.markercluster.js, MarkerCluster.css, MarkerCluster.Default.css

Sources:
  https://unpkg.com/leaflet@1.9.4/dist/
  https://unpkg.com/leaflet.markercluster@1.5.3/dist/
